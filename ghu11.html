Pairing Steps (interactive)

  1. 1
    Start scan
    Open the browser device picker to find the scooter.
  2. 2
    Request device
    Browser asks permission to access Bluetooth devices.
  3. 3
    Connect
    Attempting to connect to the scooter's GATT server.
  4. 4
    Waiting for pairing input
    If required, press the scooter power button once to enable pairing.
  5. 5
    Subscribe to telemetry
    Listening for notifications from the scooter (speed, battery, temp).
  6. ✓
    Paired
    Connection established and telemetry streaming.
Tip: Press the scooter power button once, then click Scan & Connect.
